Coherence: the summary is well structured and well organized; its sentences form a coherent body of information about the article rather than a list of loosely related facts.
Consistency: the summary contains only information that is factually entailed by the article; it introduces no unsupported claims.
Fluency: the sentences of the summary are grammatical, well formed and easy to read.
Relevance: the summary includes the important information of the article and avoids redundant or unimportant content.
